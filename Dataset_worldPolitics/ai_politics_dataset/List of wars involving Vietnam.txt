**List of Wars Involving Vietnam**

The **list of wars involving Vietnam** spans over two millennia and reflects the country's complex and turbulent history shaped by foreign invasions, colonialism, national liberation struggles, and regional conflicts. Located in Southeast Asia, Vietnam has long been a strategic and contested territory, facing invasions from powerful neighbors such as China and regional empires, as well as European colonial powers in the modern era. In the 20th century, Vietnam played a central role in global Cold War geopolitics.

Vietnam's military history includes prolonged resistance against foreign domination, civil wars between dynastic factions, and modern ideological conflicts. These wars have significantly influenced Vietnam’s national identity, political development, and international relations.

---

### Ancient and Medieval Conflicts

#### **Chinese Domination and Resistance (111 BCE–938 CE)**

* Vietnam, then known as variously as Jiaozhi or Giao Chỉ, was under direct Chinese rule for over a millennium.
* **Notable rebellions**: The Trung Sisters’ rebellion (40–43 CE) and Lady Triệu’s uprising (248 CE) became legendary in Vietnamese history.
* **Battle of Bạch Đằng (938)**: Vietnamese forces under Ngô Quyền defeated the Southern Han, ending Chinese domination and establishing Vietnamese independence.

#### **Lý, Trần, and Lê Dynasties (11th–15th centuries)**

* Vietnam faced several **invasions from the Mongol Yuan dynasty** in the 13th century. The Trần dynasty repelled these invasions in 1258, 1285, and 1288.
* The **Lam Sơn uprising (1418–1427)** led by Lê Lợi resulted in the expulsion of Ming Chinese occupiers and the founding of the Lê dynasty.

---

### Early Modern Period

#### **Internal Conflicts: Trịnh–Nguyễn and Tây Sơn Wars (16th–18th centuries)**

* Vietnam experienced protracted internal conflict between the northern Trịnh Lords and southern Nguyễn Lords.
* The **Tây Sơn Rebellion (1771–1802)** overthrew both factions temporarily and led to civil war, culminating in Nguyễn Ánh (Emperor Gia Long) founding the Nguyễn dynasty in 1802.

---

### Colonial Era and Anti-Colonial Struggles

#### **French Conquest of Vietnam (1858–1884)**

* France gradually colonized Vietnam, making it part of **French Indochina**.
* Resistance came from figures like Phan Đình Phùng and the Cần Vương movement, although these efforts were ultimately suppressed.

#### **First Indochina War (1946–1954)**

* **Belligerents**: Việt Minh (led by Hồ Chí Minh) vs. France
* **Context**: After Japan’s World War II surrender, Vietnamese nationalists sought independence from returning French colonial forces.
* **Outcome**: Việt Minh victory at the **Battle of Điện Biên Phủ (1954)** led to the Geneva Accords and temporary division of Vietnam at the 17th parallel.

---

### Cold War Conflicts

#### **Vietnam War / Second Indochina War (1955–1975)**

* **Belligerents**: North Vietnam and the Viet Cong vs. South Vietnam, the United States, and allied forces (e.g., Australia, South Korea)
* **Context**: A Cold War conflict over ideology, with North Vietnam seeking reunification under communism and the South supported by anti-communist powers.
* **Major events**: Tet Offensive (1968), U.S. withdrawal (1973), Fall of Saigon (1975)
* **Outcome**: North Vietnamese victory and reunification of Vietnam as the Socialist Republic of Vietnam in 1976.

---

### Post-Unification Conflicts

#### **Cambodian–Vietnamese War (1978–1989)**

* **Belligerents**: Vietnam vs. Khmer Rouge regime of Cambodia
* **Context**: Vietnam invaded Cambodia in late 1978 in response to border attacks and genocide under Pol Pot. Vietnamese forces overthrew the Khmer Rouge and installed a friendly government.
* **Outcome**: Prolonged occupation and insurgency; Vietnam withdrew in 1989 under international pressure.

#### **Sino–Vietnamese War (1979)**

* **Belligerents**: China vs. Vietnam
* **Context**: In retaliation for Vietnam’s invasion of Cambodia, China launched a punitive invasion of northern Vietnam.
* **Outcome**: Both sides claimed victory. The war was brief but bloody and led to a decade of tense border skirmishes.

#### **Sino–Vietnamese Border Conflicts (1979–1990)**

* A series of low-intensity but persistent clashes continued along the China–Vietnam border, concluding with normalization of relations in the early 1990s.

---

### Participation in Peacekeeping and International Missions

Since the 2000s, Vietnam has become increasingly involved in **UN peacekeeping operations**, reflecting its integration into the international system. Vietnamese personnel have served in missions in South Sudan, the Central African Republic, and elsewhere.

---

### Conclusion

The **list of wars involving Vietnam** demonstrates the country’s long history of military engagement shaped by geography, colonialism, internal division, and ideological struggle. From resisting powerful empires to navigating Cold War superpower rivalries, Vietnam's military past has deeply influenced its national consciousness and foreign policy. Today, Vietnam maintains a policy of peaceful development and multilateralism, while honoring a tradition of self-reliance forged through centuries of conflict.
