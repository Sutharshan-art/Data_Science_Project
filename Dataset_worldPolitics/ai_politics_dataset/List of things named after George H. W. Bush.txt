**List of Things Named After George H. W. Bush**

The **list of things named after George H. W. Bush** includes buildings, infrastructure, public institutions, and honors commemorating the 41st president of the United States, George Herbert Walker Bush (1989–1993). A prominent figure in American political history, Bush served as vice president under Ronald Reagan, director of the Central Intelligence Agency, U.S. ambassador to the United Nations, and a member of Congress before ascending to the presidency. His legacy is reflected in numerous dedications across the United States and internationally.

These namings recognize Bush’s lifetime of public service, his leadership during the end of the Cold War, his role in managing the Gulf War of 1991, and his emphasis on diplomacy, coalition-building, and voluntarism through initiatives like “Points of Light.” Honoring both his military service in World War II and his statesmanship, these commemorations are a testament to his enduring impact on American civic life and foreign policy.

---

### Historical and Political Context

George H. W. Bush assumed the presidency during a period of significant global transformation. His administration oversaw the peaceful end of the Cold War, the reunification of Germany, and the dissolution of the Soviet Union. Domestically, he faced economic challenges and growing partisanship, but maintained a reputation for prudence, diplomatic skill, and dedication to public service.

Following his presidency, Bush became widely respected across party lines for his civility, modest leadership style, and commitment to international cooperation. As a result, his name has been attached to various institutions that reflect the values of service, diplomacy, education, and national unity.

---

### Educational Institutions

#### **The George Bush School of Government and Public Service – Texas A\&M University**

Established in 1997, the Bush School is located at Texas A\&M University in College Station, Texas. The school offers graduate programs in public administration, international affairs, and homeland security. It was founded in line with Bush’s belief in preparing future leaders for careers in public service, diplomacy, and government.

#### **George H. W. Bush Presidential Library and Museum**

Also situated at Texas A\&M, this presidential library is part of the National Archives and opened in 1997. It houses documents, artifacts, and exhibits related to Bush’s presidency and earlier public service roles. It serves as both a research facility and a public educational center dedicated to understanding his leadership and era.

---

### Infrastructure and Transportation

#### **George Bush Intercontinental Airport – Houston, Texas**

Formerly Houston Intercontinental Airport, the facility was renamed in 1997 to honor Bush’s longstanding ties to Houston and his presidency. It is one of the busiest airports in the United States and serves as a key international gateway, symbolizing Bush’s global engagement.

#### **USS George H.W. Bush (CVN-77)**

Commissioned in 2009, the USS George H.W. Bush is a Nimitz-class nuclear-powered aircraft carrier in the U.S. Navy. The ship commemorates Bush’s service as a Navy aviator during World War II and his status as a war hero. Its naming marked the first time an aircraft carrier was named after a living former president.

#### **George H. W. Bush Turnpike – Texas**

This major toll highway in the Dallas–Fort Worth area was named in honor of Bush in recognition of his national infrastructure and transportation policies. It forms part of the President George Bush Turnpike system and underscores his connection to the state of Texas.

---

### Statues and Memorials

#### **George H. W. Bush Statue – Houston, Texas**

Erected in 2004 at Sesquicentennial Park in downtown Houston, this bronze statue depicts Bush in a diplomatic pose, reflecting his legacy as a consensus-builder and global leader.

#### **Bush Monument – Washington, D.C.**

In 2011, a bust of Bush was added to the U.S. Capitol's rotunda as part of the Presidential Portrait Collection, symbolizing his role in late 20th-century American governance.

---

### Honors and Awards Named After Bush

#### **George H. W. Bush Points of Light Award**

Administered by the Points of Light Foundation, this award honors individuals and organizations for outstanding voluntary service. It was inspired by Bush’s famous “thousand points of light” phrase, used to describe the value of grassroots civic engagement.

#### **George H. W. Bush Volunteerism Award**

Various civic and nonprofit organizations have established volunteerism awards in Bush’s name to promote public service and community involvement, in keeping with his lifelong advocacy for civic responsibility.

---

### International Recognition

* **George H. W. Bush Street – Tirana, Albania**
  In recognition of his support for Albanian independence and post-Cold War democratization efforts, a major street in Albania’s capital is named after Bush.

* **Bush Hill – Kuwait**
  A commemorative site in Kuwait acknowledges Bush’s leadership during the Gulf War and the liberation of Kuwait in 1991. The country has expressed deep gratitude for his defense of its sovereignty.

---

### Conclusion

The **list of things named after George H. W. Bush** reflects the breadth of his influence on American and global politics. Honoring his military valor, diplomatic acumen, and emphasis on public service, these dedications serve not only as memorials but as living institutions that continue to promote the values he championed. From academic centers and infrastructure to international tributes, the legacy of George H. W. Bush endures through the institutions that bear his name.
