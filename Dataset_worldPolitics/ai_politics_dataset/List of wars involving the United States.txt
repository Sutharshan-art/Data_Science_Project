**List of Wars Involving the United States**

The **list of wars involving the United States** comprises the major military conflicts in which the United States has participated since its founding in 1776. As one of the world’s most influential political and military powers, the United States has been involved in a wide range of conflicts—ranging from its war of independence to global wars, regional interventions, and counterinsurgency operations. These wars have shaped not only American foreign and domestic policy but also the international order.

The U.S. Constitution grants Congress the power to declare war, though presidents have frequently authorized military actions without formal declarations. The scope and scale of U.S. military involvement have varied, from limited engagements to full-scale mobilizations. The United States has played both defensive and offensive roles in wars, with motivations ranging from protecting national security to promoting ideological or humanitarian goals.

---

### 18th Century

#### **American Revolutionary War (1775–1783)**

* **Opponents:** Great Britain vs. American colonists (with France, Spain, and the Dutch Republic later allied with the colonies)
* **Context:** A colonial rebellion against British rule, leading to the independence of the United States.
* **Outcome:** Treaty of Paris (1783) recognized U.S. sovereignty.

---

### 19th Century

#### **War of 1812 (1812–1815)**

* **Opponents:** United States vs. Great Britain
* **Context:** Triggered by maritime disputes and British interference in American trade and expansion.
* **Outcome:** Status quo ante bellum; boosted American nationalism.

#### **Mexican–American War (1846–1848)**

* **Opponents:** United States vs. Mexico
* **Context:** Resulted from territorial disputes after the U.S. annexation of Texas.
* **Outcome:** U.S. acquired vast territories including California and the Southwest under the Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo.

#### **American Civil War (1861–1865)**

* **Opponents:** Union (United States) vs. Confederate States
* **Context:** Fought over secession and slavery. The most devastating war in U.S. history.
* **Outcome:** Union victory, abolition of slavery, and preservation of the United States.

#### **Spanish–American War (1898)**

* **Opponents:** United States vs. Spain
* **Context:** Sparked by U.S. support for Cuban independence and the sinking of the USS *Maine*.
* **Outcome:** U.S. acquired overseas territories including Puerto Rico, Guam, and the Philippines.

---

### 20th Century

#### **World War I (1917–1918)**

* **Opponents:** Allied Powers (including the U.S.) vs. Central Powers
* **Context:** U.S. entered late in the war, citing German submarine warfare and the Zimmerman Telegram.
* **Outcome:** Allied victory; Treaty of Versailles and U.S. emergence as a global power.

#### **World War II (1941–1945)**

* **Opponents:** Allied Powers (including the U.S.) vs. Axis Powers
* **Context:** U.S. joined after the attack on Pearl Harbor. Played a decisive role in both the European and Pacific theaters.
* **Outcome:** Total victory for the Allies; U.S. became a superpower and founding member of the United Nations.

#### **Korean War (1950–1953)**

* **Opponents:** United States and UN forces vs. North Korea and China
* **Context:** A Cold War conflict to contain communism in Korea.
* **Outcome:** Armistice signed; Korea remains divided.

#### **Vietnam War (U.S. involvement: 1955–1975)**

* **Opponents:** United States and South Vietnam vs. North Vietnam and Viet Cong
* **Context:** Part of Cold War containment strategy.
* **Outcome:** U.S. withdrawal and eventual North Vietnamese victory; major domestic unrest in the U.S.

#### **Gulf War (1990–1991)**

* **Opponents:** U.S.-led coalition vs. Iraq
* **Context:** Response to Iraq’s invasion of Kuwait.
* **Outcome:** Coalition victory; limited U.S. presence remained in the region.

---

### 21st Century

#### **War in Afghanistan (2001–2021)**

* **Opponents:** United States and NATO vs. Taliban, al-Qaeda
* **Context:** Launched in response to the September 11 attacks.
* **Outcome:** Taliban overthrown in 2001; U.S. withdrawal in 2021 followed by Taliban return to power.

#### **Iraq War (2003–2011)**

* **Opponents:** United States and allies vs. Saddam Hussein’s regime and insurgents
* **Context:** Alleged weapons of mass destruction and links to terrorism.
* **Outcome:** Saddam removed from power; prolonged insurgency and regional instability.

#### **Global War on Terror (2001–present)**

* **Scope:** Includes operations in Pakistan, Somalia, Yemen, Syria, and elsewhere.
* **Context:** Counterterrorism efforts against groups like al-Qaeda, ISIS, and affiliated militants.
* **Ongoing:** Involves airstrikes, drone warfare, and special operations forces.

---

### Other Military Engagements

* **Panama (1989):** U.S. invaded to depose Manuel Noriega.
* **Haiti (1994–1995):** Intervention to restore democratically elected government.
* **Libya (2011):** NATO-led intervention during civil war; U.S. provided air support.
* **Syria (2014–present):** U.S. operations against ISIS and involvement in the Syrian civil conflict.

---

### Conclusion

The **list of wars involving the United States** reflects the nation’s evolving role from a revolutionary republic to a global superpower. Over the centuries, U.S. military engagements have shifted from territorial expansion and civil conflict to international coalition-building and counterterrorism. These wars have had profound effects on U.S. foreign policy, military doctrine, and its global standing, influencing domestic debates and shaping the modern international order.
