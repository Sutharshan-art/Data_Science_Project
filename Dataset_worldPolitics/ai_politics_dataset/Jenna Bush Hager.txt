**Jenna Bush Hager** (née Jenna Welch Bush; born November 25, 1981) is an American news personality, author, and advocate, known for her work on NBC’s *Today* show and her philanthropic initiatives focusing on literacy. The younger daughter of President George W. Bush and First Lady Laura Bush, Jenna Bush Hager’s career reflects the evolving role of presidential offspring in public life and the interplay between media, politics, and social advocacy in early 21st-century America.

### Early Life and Education

Jenna Bush was born in Dallas, Texas, the second twin daughter of George W. Bush, then the Governor of Texas, and Laura Bush, a former librarian and reading advocate. Alongside her twin sister, Barbara, she spent her childhood in the public eye as her father was elected the 43rd President of the United States in 2000. As First Daughters, Jenna and Barbara attended private school while their mother oversaw their academic and social development in the White House. Jenna graduated from Preston Hollow Elementary, then moved with her family to Washington, D.C., where she attended the prestigious private Sidwell Friends School. She later earned a Bachelor of Arts in English from the University of Texas at Austin in 2004.

### Post–White House Career Beginnings

Following the conclusion of her father’s presidency in January 2009, Jenna Bush returned to private life and pursued a master’s degree in education at the University of Texas at Austin, reflecting the family’s longstanding interest in literacy and education policy. She completed her degree in 2012 and briefly taught middle-school English in a low-income public school in Baltimore, Maryland, gaining firsthand experience with the challenges of urban education reform and the disparities in literacy outcomes—issues her mother had championed as First Lady.

### Media Career and *Today* Show

In December 2012, Bush Hager joined NBC News as a contributor to the *Today* show, debuting her segment “Read with Jenna,” which promotes children’s books and literacy. By April 2015, she was named a full-time correspondent and co-host of *Today with Kathie Lee and Hoda* (later *Hoda & Jenna*), appearing on the fourth hour of *Today*. Her media presence broadened to include regular appearances on *TODAY All Day* and special broadcasts from the Gulf Coast following Hurricane Katrina anniversaries, where she highlighted long-term recovery efforts and federal disaster relief policy.

### Philanthropy and Advocacy

Bush Hager’s public profile has been closely tied to literacy advocacy, mirroring Laura Bush’s initiatives as First Lady. In 2014 she co-founded the online reading platform “Read with Jenna,” partnering with Scholastic and local libraries to distribute free copies of selected books to schools and community centers. Through NBCUniversal and corporate sponsors, the program has delivered over 250,000 books nationwide, aiming to address reading proficiency gaps identified by federal assessments such as the National Assessment of Educational Progress (NAEP). Jenna has spoken before congressional subcommittees and at Education Nation summits on the role of early reading interventions in improving graduation rates and workforce readiness.

### Political and Social Context

As a post-First Daughter entering the media and nonprofit sectors, Bush Hager represents a newer model of political offspring who leverage family prominence toward focused issue advocacy rather than partisan politics. Her work occurs against a backdrop of bipartisan debates over federal education funding, the Every Student Succeeds Act (2015), and the role of public–private partnerships in schooling. While largely nonpartisan, her initiatives have intersected with policy discussions on Title I funding, library budgets, and federal literacy grants administered by the Department of Education.

### Authorship and Publications

In addition to her television work, Jenna Bush Hager is the author of several books aimed at children and families. Her 2016 memoir, *Ana’s Story: A Journey of Hope*—co-written with her twin sister, Barbara—chronicles their experiences volunteering in Latin America and discusses the impact of global health and social development programs supported by USAID and the Peace Corps. In 2018 she published the children’s book *Sweet Home Louisiana*, which celebrates the culture and heritage of the American South. These publications contribute to conversations on soft power and cultural diplomacy by highlighting American civic engagement abroad.

### Personal Life

In May 2008, Jenna Bush married Henry Chase Hager, a former campaign aide to Senator John Kerry. They have three children and reside in McLean, Virginia. Her personal experiences as a working mother inform her occasional commentary on early childhood education policy and family–work balance, raising issues central to debates over federal parental-leave legislation and childcare subsidies.

### Legacy and Influence

Jenna Bush Hager’s career underscores the expanding avenues through which former presidential family members shape public policy and civic life in the 21st century. By combining media influence, educational expertise, and nonpartisan advocacy, she has carved a niche in the national dialogue on literacy and youth development, illustrating the enduring intersection of politics, philanthropy, and popular culture in American society.
