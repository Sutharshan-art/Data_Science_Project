### Early Life and Career

Andrea Riseborough was born on 20 November 1981 in Newcastle upon Tyne, England. She trained at the **Royal Academy of Dramatic Art (RADA)**, graduating in 2005. Riseborough began her career with stage performances before transitioning to television and film, gaining recognition for her versatile acting skills.
