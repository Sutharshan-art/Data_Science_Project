**3 Remixes for the New Cold War**

**Overview**
*3 Remixes for the New Cold War* is an EP of remixed tracks released in 1998 by the Canadian synthpop band Rational Youth. Issued on July 21, 1998, through the Coboloid Music label, it features three remixes drawn from the band’s earlier material, updated to reflect late‑1990s electronic production trends ([reddit.com][1]).

**Background and Release**
Rational Youth, formed in 1981, were pioneers in integrating synthesizer-driven pop with politically minded lyrics. The *Cold War Night Life* era, reflecting late‑Cold War tensions, set the scene for later reinterpretations. Nearly two decades later, *3 Remixes for the New Cold War* revisited their early catalog, offering modernized versions of their emblematic themes in an age witnessing renewed geopolitical frictions

By presenting electronic reinterpretations of tracks rooted in early 1980s geopolitical anxiety, the EP subtly comments on the cyclical nature of international rivalries.

Rather than offering overt political content, the release leverages musical form and retooling to provoke reflection on persistent global tensions.
