**Bulgarian Revolutionary Central Committee**

The **Bulgarian Revolutionary Central Committee** (BRCC) was a political organization founded in 1869 with the primary aim of achieving the liberation of Bulgaria from Ottoman rule. It played a pivotal role in organizing and coordinating revolutionary activities during the late 19th century and was instrumental in shaping the Bulgarian national liberation movement. The committee’s efforts culminated in uprisings that contributed to the eventual establishment of an autonomous Bulgarian state.

---

### Historical and Political Context

In the 19th century, Bulgaria was part of the Ottoman Empire, experiencing centuries of foreign domination. The rise of nationalism across Europe inspired Bulgarian intellectuals and revolutionaries to seek independence and national revival.

The Ottoman Empire’s weakening grip, coupled with the growing influence of the Great Powers and the spread of revolutionary ideas, created fertile ground for nationalist movements. The Bulgarian struggle for autonomy became part of a wider pattern of Balkan nationalist uprisings during this period.

---

### Formation and Goals

The Bulgarian Revolutionary Central Committee was established in Bucharest, Romania, in 1869 by Bulgarian émigrés and revolutionaries, including prominent figures such as Lyuben Karavelov and Hristo Botev. It functioned as a clandestine revolutionary body tasked with preparing the Bulgarian population for armed resistance against Ottoman rule.

The BRCC aimed to unite various Bulgarian revolutionary groups and coordinate efforts to awaken national consciousness and organize insurrections. Its ultimate goal was the liberation of Bulgaria and the creation of a free and independent Bulgarian state.

---

### Activities and Revolutionary Work

The committee engaged in political agitation, dissemination of revolutionary literature, and the training of insurgents. It sought to mobilize support among the Bulgarian population both inside and outside Ottoman territories.

The BRCC was closely involved in the planning and execution of the **April Uprising of 1876**, a major armed rebellion against Ottoman authorities. Although the uprising was brutally suppressed, it drew international attention to the Bulgarian cause and generated widespread sympathy in Europe.

---

### International Impact and Support

The suppression of the April Uprising and subsequent Ottoman atrocities, known as the **Bulgarian Horrors**, provoked outrage in Western Europe and contributed to the growing interventionist policies of Russia and other Great Powers in the Balkans.

The BRCC’s activities helped to internationalize the Bulgarian liberation struggle, linking it to the larger Eastern Question and the decline of Ottoman influence in Europe.

---

### Decline and Legacy

Following the Russo-Turkish War (1877–1878) and the subsequent Treaty of Berlin, which established the autonomous Principality of Bulgaria, the role of the BRCC diminished as formal state structures replaced revolutionary organizations.

Nonetheless, the BRCC’s legacy persisted as a symbol of Bulgarian nationalism and resistance. Its members and ideals influenced later political movements and contributed to the shaping of modern Bulgarian identity.

---

### Conclusion

The Bulgarian Revolutionary Central Committee was a central force in Bulgaria’s national liberation movement during the late Ottoman period. Through its coordination of revolutionary activity and promotion of national consciousness, it played a crucial role in the eventual establishment of Bulgarian autonomy. The BRCC remains an important chapter in the history of Balkan nationalism and the decline of Ottoman authority in Europe.
