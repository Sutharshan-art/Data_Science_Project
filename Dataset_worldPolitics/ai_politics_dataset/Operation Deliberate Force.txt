**Operation Deliberate Force**

**Operation Deliberate Force** was a sustained NATO air campaign conducted between 30 August and 20 September 1995 against Bosnian Serb military targets in the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina. It marked NATO’s first large-scale combat operation and was pivotal in coercing Bosnian Serb forces to enter negotiations that culminated in the Dayton Peace Accords.

## Historical and Political Context

Following Bosnia and Herzegovina’s 1992 declaration of independence, Bosnian Serb forces—backed by the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia—initiated a campaign of ethnic cleansing against Bosniak (Bosnian Muslim) and Croat civilians. Despite United Nations Security Council resolutions and the deployment of UNPROFOR peacekeepers, besieged enclaves such as Sarajevo and safe areas like Srebrenica remained under constant artillery and sniper fire. The 28 August 1995 Markale marketplace shelling in Sarajevo—killing scores of civilians—generated international outrage and prompted UN and NATO leaders to consider robust military enforcement of UN mandates.

## Planning and Decision Making

In early August 1995, UNPROFOR commander General Sir Michael Rose requested stronger airpower “to make the price of continued aggression too high for the Bosnian Serbs.” After the second Markale massacre, U.S. Secretary of State Warren Christopher convened an emergency meeting of NATO foreign ministers. On 28 August, the North Atlantic Council authorized NATO to target Bosnian Serb military infrastructure, command posts, artillery positions, and ammunition depots that threatened UN-designated safe areas and UN forces. Operation Deliberate Force was coordinated with a ground offensive (Operation Storm) by Croatian forces in western Bosnia, creating a two-front crisis for Bosnian Serb units.

## Execution of the Campaign

NATO began strikes on 30 August 1995, employing U.S. Air Force B-52 and B-1 bombers, French Mirage 2000Ds, British Tornado GR.1s, and other allied aircraft. Over 19 days, NATO aircraft flew more than 3,500 sorties and delivered approximately 4,000 precision-guided munitions. Targets included:

* Artillery and multiple-launch rocket systems around Sarajevo and Banja Luka
* Command and control centers at Pale, the Bosnian Serb political capital
* Ammunition and fuel storage sites supporting offensives in the so-called “Safe Areas”

In parallel, NATO enforced a no-fly zone and maritime embargo (Operation Sharp Guard) in the Adriatic to interdict smuggling of arms and materiel. Close coordination between NATO and UNPROFOR ground observers improved target selection and helped avoid civilian casualties, though some incidents of collateral damage provoked criticism.

## Impact and Outcomes

Operation Deliberate Force significantly degraded Bosnian Serb military capabilities and eroded the political will of Radovan Karadžić and General Ratko Mladić. Within days, Bosnian Serb forces suspended offensive operations in Sarajevo and the safe areas. The campaign’s military pressure, combined with gains by Croatian and Bosniak ground forces, compelled all parties to accept U.S.-brokered peace talks at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base and thereafter at Dayton, Ohio. The resulting **General Framework Agreement for Peace in Bosnia and Herzegovina** was initialed on 21 November and formally signed on 14 December 1995.

## Political Significance and Legacy

Operation Deliberate Force demonstrated NATO’s post–Cold War role in crisis management and humanitarian enforcement, shifting the alliance from collective territorial defense to expeditionary intervention. Politically, the operation restored credibility to UN mandates, deterred further large-scale aggression in Bosnia, and set precedents for airpower’s coercive application in crises (e.g., Kosovo 1999, Libya 2011). Critics, however, questioned the proportionality of NATO’s response and raised concerns about sovereignty and UN Security Council authority.

In Bosnia and Herzegovina, Deliberate Force is widely regarded as a turning point that ended the stalemate of 1992–1995 and facilitated the implementation of peace. The operation underscored the efficacy of precision-guided munitions in reducing civilian casualties and highlighted the importance of integrated air-ground coordination. NATO’s institutional learning from Deliberate Force informed subsequent doctrinal developments, including the establishment of the Allied Rapid Reaction Corps and improved civil-military planning for humanitarian operations.
