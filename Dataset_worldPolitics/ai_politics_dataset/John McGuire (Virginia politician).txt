**John Joseph McGuire III** (born August 24, 1968) is an American politician, businessman, and former United States Navy SEAL serving as the U.S. Representative for Virginia’s 5th Congressional District since January 3, 2025. A member of the Republican Party, McGuire previously represented the 10th District in the Virginia State Senate (2024–2025) and the 56th District in the Virginia House of Delegates (2018–2024).

### Early Life and Military Service

Born in Richmond, Virginia, McGuire was raised in Central Virginia and spent part of his youth in the foster care system. He graduated from Northwest Florida State College before enlisting in the U.S. Navy in 1988. McGuire served for a decade as a Navy SEAL, deploying on classified missions and receiving multiple commendations for his service. After leaving active duty in 1998, he entered private business, founding and operating companies in the security, fitness, and consulting sectors.

### Virginia House of Delegates (2018–2024)

McGuire launched his political career in 2017, winning a six-candidate Republican primary for the 56th House District, a suburban–rural constituency north and west of Richmond. He defeated Democratic nominee Melissa Dart in the general election by a 60%–40% margin, despite a Democratic wave in the state legislature that year. During his three terms, McGuire focused on veterans’ affairs, public safety, education funding, and balancing the state budget without tax increases.

His notable legislative achievements included:

* **Veterans’ Identification**: Authored a law allowing veterans’ status to be indicated on Virginia driver’s licenses and ID cards.
* **Teacher Compensation**: Secured a 5% pay raise for public‐school teachers in the 2019–2020 budget.
* **Opioid Crisis Response**: Co-sponsored measures expanding access to addiction treatment and naloxone distribution.

### Virginia State Senate (2024–2025)

In 2023, McGuire ran for the newly redrawn 10th State Senate District, which combined parts of his former House constituency with areas of suburban Richmond and the Virginia Piedmont. He won the seat over Democratic contender Chuck Smith, campaigning on jobs creation, school safety, and support for law enforcement. Although his tenure in the Senate lasted less than a year—ending upon his resignation to assume his U.S. House seat—he served on the Senate Education and Health Committee and the Finance and Appropriations Committee, contributing to debates on broadband expansion and mental‐health services.

### U.S. House of Representatives (2025–Present)

In June 2024, McGuire narrowly defeated incumbent Congressman Bob Good in the Republican primary for Virginia’s 5th District by 370 votes out of more than 62,000 cast, following an automatic recount. He then won the general election against Democratic nominee Gloria Witt, carrying a district that spans southern and central Virginia, including Staunton, Lynchburg, and Danville.

Upon taking office in the 119th Congress, McGuire was assigned to the House Armed Services Committee and the House Committee on Oversight and Accountability. He has stated priorities of strengthening national defense, addressing drug trafficking along rural highways, and promoting economic development in post‐industrial communities. He has also signaled support for competitive federal hiring and increased military-to‐civilian career pathways for veterans.

### Political Context and Positions

McGuire’s ascent reflects broader trends in Virginia politics—suburban growth, a shrinking rural electorate, and shifting party alignments following the 2020 presidential election. His narrow primary victory over a member of the hard‐right Freedom Caucus underscores ideological tensions within Virginia’s Republican Party between traditional conservatives and more populist, Trump‐aligned factions.

Policy positions he has advocated include:

* **Fiscal Conservatism**: Opposing tax increases and calling for budget restraint at the federal level.
* **Veterans’ Issues**: Expanding healthcare and employment benefits for veterans.
* **Education**: Supporting increased local control of schools and targeted funding for workforce‐training programs.
* **Public Safety**: Backing federal grants to bolster rural law‐enforcement staffing and cross‐jurisdictional drug interdiction efforts.

### Personal Life

McGuire is married to Tracy McGuire; they have five children. He remains active in veterans’ organizations and local service groups. His Christian faith and background as a Navy SEAL inform his emphasis on leadership, resilience, and community service.

### Electoral History

* **2017**: Elected to Virginia House of Delegates, District 56
* **2019 & 2021**: Re-elected to House of Delegates
* **2023**: Elected to Virginia State Senate, District 10
* **2024**: Elected to U.S. House of Representatives, Virginia’s 5th District.
