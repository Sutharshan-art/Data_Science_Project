The Soviet Union (SU; Russian: Сове́тский Сою́з, romanized: Sovetsky Soyuz, lit. 'Council Union'; СС) or Union of Soviet Socialists Republics (USSR; Russian: СССР) was a 20th-century country based about Russia.
Soviet Union or Sovetsky Soyuz (Russian: Сове́тский Сою́з) may also refer to:

Sovetsky Soyuz (Russian: Сове́тский Сою́з; Soviet Union), the Soviet magazine Soviet Union
Sovetsky Soyuz-class battleship (Russian: Сове́тский Сою́з; Soviet Union), the Soviet battleship class Soviet Union
Soviet battleship Sovetsky Soyuz (Russian: Советский Союз; Soviet Union), the battleship Soviet Union, lead ship of the eponymous class; see Sovetsky Soyuz-class battleship
SS Sovetskiy Soyuz (Russian: Советский Союз; Soviet Union), the SS Soviet Union, the name of passenger-cargo ship SS Albert Ballin from 1949 to 1980
A national sports team of the Soviet Union
